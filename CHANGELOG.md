# Changelog

## 2.1.2

- Added colorful accents to Home, Settings, setup instructions and note actions.
- Gave GitHub, Patreon and feedback separate tap targets, with bundled brand icons and a compact Patreon shortcut beside Settings.
- Enlarged the creator signature and added a random emoji every 0.4 seconds, respecting Reduce Motion and app activity.
- Added in-app email feedback with an external-mail option and a copy-address fallback.
- Darkened the dark-mode handwriting canvas and added a persistent dotted-paper toggle in Settings and the editor. Guides follow scrolling and stay out of exports and Notion sync.

## 2.1.1

- Expanded the note icon collection and added a searchable picker with persistent choices.
- Added selected-note animation with Reduce Motion support.
- Added title search, sorting, pinned notes, and Recently Deleted with restore.
- Added creator information and email feedback in Settings.
- Updated light and dark app icons, documentation, and MIT licensing.

## Earlier releases

Introduced local PencilKit notes, editable backups, Notion image sync, sidebar selection, bulk actions, and automated unsigned IPA releases.

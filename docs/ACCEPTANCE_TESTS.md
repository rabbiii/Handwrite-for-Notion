# Device release checklist

- Create, edit, close, and reopen a note; confirm strokes survive relaunch.
- Choose an icon, rename the note, export and restore its backup, and verify the icon persists.
- Confirm only the open note animates and Reduce Motion stops animation.
- Search, sort, pin, and bulk-select notes; tapping filenames must select in Edit mode.
- Delete a note, relaunch, restore it, and verify its handwriting. Confirm permanent deletion requires approval.
- Check light and dark app icons, large text, VoiceOver labels, and compact layouts.
- Connect a test Notion page, sync edits, and verify unrelated content remains unchanged.
- Export backups before testing installation or signing changes.

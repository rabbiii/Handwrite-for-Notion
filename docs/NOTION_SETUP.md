# Connect Notion

1. Open [Notion integrations](https://www.notion.so/profile/integrations) and create an internal integration for your workspace.
2. Enable read, insert, and update content capabilities.
3. On the destination Notion page, open the page menu and add your integration under Connections.
4. Paste the integration token into PencilNotes Settings and connect.
5. Open a note, choose its Notion destination, and tap Sync.

Search only finds pages available to your integration. If a page is missing, check its connection and workspace permissions.

Sync uploads a PNG and updates the linked image block. Editable strokes remain on the iPad. Auto Sync is optional and requires an active app and internet connection. Failed or uncertain requests may require your attention before retrying. Check the destination before resetting a link to avoid duplicate images.

Keep the token private. Disconnect in Settings to remove the saved credential. Revoke it in Notion if it has been exposed.
